# Aura 45-Second Remotion Promo: The Master Animation Plan

To make the video truly cinematic and "cool enough" for a high-end SaaS launch, we need to treat each slide as a distinct, motion-rich scene. Here is the frame-by-frame animation plan for the 45-second promo (1350 frames at 30fps).

## Scene 1: The Hero (0s - 5s)
*   **Entrance:** Deep blur (20px) resolves to sharp focus over 1 second.
*   **Camera:** Continuous, slow 3D zoom (1.0x to 1.1x) with a very subtle Y-axis rotation (-5deg to 0deg).
*   **Action:** The curl command in the terminal types out character-by-character.
*   **Highlight:** A rough, hand-drawn emerald marker (Rough.js) sweeps behind the words *"Semantic Time Machine"*.

## Scene 2: The Next 30 Years (5s - 10s)
*   **Entrance:** Fast horizontal wipe transition.
*   **Action:** The text *"99% of code"* flickers with a digital glitch effect (rapid opacity/translation shifts) to emphasize the chaotic AI future.
*   **Highlight:** The "Aura's Core Thesis" box slides up from the bottom with a smooth spring animation, drawing focus to the solution.

## Scene 3: The Flaw - Pan & Reveal (10s - 17s)
*   **Camera:** Starts tightly framed on the left box ("Git tracks text"). 
*   **Action 1:** The text `== Fatal Merge Conflict ==` pulses red like a warning siren.
*   **Camera Move:** At 13s, a heavy, cinematic spring-pan moves the camera aggressively to the right box ("Aura tracks logic").
*   **Action 2:** The green checkmarks (`✓`) pop in sequentially with a satisfying scaling bounce.

## Scene 4: The Semantic Scalpel (17s - 23s)
*   **Entrance:** The "Aura Rewind" Mac terminal scales up from the center of the screen (0.8x to 1.0x) with a heavy shadow.
*   **Action:** The terminal output simulates a real-time scan. The text `🔍 Scanning Merkle-Graph...` appears, pauses, and then the success message `✓ Function reverted` flashes emerald green.

## Scene 5: The Amnesia Protocol (23s - 28s)
*   **Entrance:** Glitch transition into the slide.
*   **Action:** When the text `[SYSTEM: AURA OVERRIDE]` appears, the entire composition shakes slightly (simulating a heavy impact or "memory wipe"). The background subtly shifts from a dark red tint back to pure black/emerald to signify the "reset."

## Scene 6: The Gatekeeper Block (28s - 33s)
*   **Entrance:** Fade in. 
*   **Action:** The terminal shows the `git commit` command. After 1 second, a massive, slightly tilted red `✗ BLOCKED` overlay slams onto the screen, accompanied by a rapid camera shake.

## Scene 7: The AI Consensus Cascade (33s - 39s)
*   **Entrance:** Smooth slide up.
*   **Action:** The three AI testimonials (Gemini, Claude, GPT) do not appear at once. They cascade into view sequentially from left to right, fading up and sliding into place over 2 seconds.

## Scene 8: The Drop & CTA (39s - 45s)
*   **Entrance:** Hard cut. 
*   **Action:** The words *"Open Source. 100% Local."* scale down dramatically from massive (2.0x) to normal size (1.0x) and lock into place.
*   **Climax:** The Aura SVG logo dynamically draws itself (stroke animation) in the center of the screen while the final curl command glows. Fade to black.
